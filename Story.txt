rb2_2 As the newly formed creature has a staring contest with you, you feel a weird tingly sensation creeping up your right arm, in which you're holding it and slowly climbing up your back, getting lost in your hair... or so it felt. Slowly, your vision blurs, making it harder to distinguish the vague being in your hand from your own body and mind, new thoughts flood yours and cold shivers merge through your blood vessels, alien organisms. It seems what used to be the back of your skull is now a gaping wound, offering a lofty shelter to the new being living through you. You died, but your body lives on.
