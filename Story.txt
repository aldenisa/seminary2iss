Dangerous story (reading at your own risk)

1. After returning from a failed crusade, you want to go home but you encounter a fork in the road.
You can go left (go to 2) or right (go to 3).

3. Such a shame! You have encountered a wicked witch! She has 3 potions. These are: blue potion, red potion and green potion.
You can chose blue, red or green. 

6.
You choose the red potion. It's glowing omniously. Drinking it, you feel yourself becoming smaller and smaller and bam! 
You suddenly have four feet and bright red fur! You try to talk but you can't. The witch laughs evily and in a puff of smoke and wind dissapears.
You look around and see nothing new. Where do you go next? Through off the road towards the forest or down the road towards your village?

You go down the road... At sunset you arrive famished at your village. You seek out your friend, and find him in his garder. You can't ask him anything but try and eat some berries. He gets angry and starts chasing you. You flee crying. You do not know what will happen, how you will turn human again. 
Days pass, you scavenging for food with no human interaction. Soon, it becomes hard to think in words and after a week, you notice spells of time in which you have no sense of self. You think you are losing your humanity.
You do not realize when you have lost all that makes you human, emotions and thoughts. You do not remember anything. And this is the end of your story, for you are now completely and truly a fox.
